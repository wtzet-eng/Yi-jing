Privacy Policy

Yi Jing (I Ching / Book of Changes) · Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how the Yi Jing app, available as a web app and Android app, handles information when you use its hexagram reading and optional AI-guided presence session.

1. Information you choose to enter

You can cast a hexagram without creating an account. The app does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. Your question and your reading are displayed in the app.

If you use the AI-guided presence session, the text you submit in that session, your question (if provided), relevant hexagram details, and the conversation so far are sent to our server so an AI response can be generated. Please avoid entering passwords, financial details, or information you would not want processed by an AI service.

2. How information is used

Information sent through the presence session is used to generate the requested response and deliver the app's features. It may also be processed to maintain the service, protect it from misuse, and diagnose technical problems.

The app does not provide user accounts or a server-side conversation history. Reading and session messages are kept in the current app session and are not saved by the app to a personal profile or app database. Your browser may retain the display preference for dark mode in local storage. The app's service worker may cache app resources to support loading and offline use.

3. AI processing and service providers

When the AI presence feature is used, our server forwards the necessary prompt to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. Google processes that information under the terms and privacy practices applicable to the API service and account configuration. Google's handling, retention, and use of prompts may depend on the service and terms that apply.

For more information, see the Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

The app is also delivered through hosting and infrastructure providers. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device details, timestamps, and request metadata to deliver, secure, and operate the service, subject to their applicable policies and configurations.

4. Storage and retention

The app does not operate a user account system or store a server-side history of your questions or presence-session messages. Information is processed while requests are handled. Hosting, security, or AI service providers may retain technical records or submitted content for periods governed by their own terms, settings, and policies; we cannot promise immediate deletion from those providers' systems.

5. Sharing

Information is shared with Google when needed to generate an AI response, and with hosting or infrastructure providers as needed to operate the app. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or others. We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers for their own advertising purposes.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the app and its communications. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not submit information that needs to remain confidential.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. It does not require registration. If you believe a child has sent personal information through the AI feature and would like to raise a concern, please contact the developer using the contact details on the app's Google Play listing.

8. Your choices and privacy questions

You can use the hexagram-casting feature without using the AI presence session. You can also choose what you type into the app and clear locally stored browser data, including the saved display preference, through your browser or device settings.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the contact email shown on the Yi Jing app's Google Play Store listing.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app or its data practices change. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page.

This Privacy Policy applies to Yi Jing (I Ching / Book of Changes), including the web app and Android app.
